# Promises require explicit evidence-backed commitment

E.R.I.I. classifies a future statement as a Promise only when direct evidence identifies a responsible party and a sufficiently clear future action. Wishes, invitations, unaccepted proposals, and unverifiable emotional expressions may remain memories or narrative tension but do not become mechanically accountable promises; a Promise without a comparable deadline may remain in history yet cannot produce a time-based overdue signal, favoring missed ambiguity over false accusations of broken trust.
