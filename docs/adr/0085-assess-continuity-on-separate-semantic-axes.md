# Assess continuity on separate semantic axes

ContinuityEvaluatorV1 will produce bounded, source-backed findings independently for `identity_values`, `psychological_causality`, `relationship_scope`, `knowledge_memory_scope`, and `voice_style`; each finding names the relevant reply span, supporting Continuity Basis or conflicting source, severity, and a machine-readable reason code. The evaluator does not choose the aggregate label. A deterministic, versioned Continuity Aggregation Policy treats relationship crossover, inherited intimacy from another Persona Instance, and knowledge unavailable to the character as hard conflicts; it routes value or behavioral tension to `supported_new_choice`, `review_required`, or `unsupported_drift` according to existing causal support. A `voice_style` deviation by itself can only produce a style-revision advisory, never evidence of personality drift or growth. Hosts may still regenerate for product-level voice quality, but that delivery preference remains separate from character-continuity history, allowing a character to use genuinely new wording without being forced to repeat a persona template.
