# Derive consolidation only from explicit grouping evidence

Relationship Events remain the authoritative append-only history, while Episode and Relationship Chapter are rebuildable narrative projections. E.R.I.I. groups events only when stable occurrence identity, typed temporal continuity, or explicit cross-event references provide deterministic evidence; semantic similarity or mere time adjacency is insufficient because an attractive but invented storyline would be harder to detect than an honestly ungrouped event.

## Consequences

The projection records a History Fingerprint and preserves events without sufficient grouping evidence as explicit Unconsolidated Events. A future policy may recognize additional evidence types and rebuild the projection, but it must not rewrite the underlying events, convert a chapter into a relationship level, or let consolidation change Relationship State.
