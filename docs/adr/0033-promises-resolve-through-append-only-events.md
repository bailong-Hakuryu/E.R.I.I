# Promises resolve through append-only events

A Promise remains immutable and its current resolution is projected from later Promise Resolution events that record fulfillment, cancellation, supersession, or an evidenced decision not to fulfill. Passing a due time only derives a Recall Signal and never becomes a breach or relationship penalty by itself; renegotiation links the old Promise to a new one, preserving the original commitment and timing while leaving any trust, conflict, or repair effect to separate evidence-backed adjudication.
