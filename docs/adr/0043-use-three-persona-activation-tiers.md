# Use three persona activation tiers

Approved Persona Interpretation assigns each item a Foundation, Situational, or Reference activation tier. Planned recall always carries Foundation identity, inner dynamics, tensions, hard boundaries, voice rules, relationship premise, and their minimum formative dependency closure; Situational material is selected deterministically from the query, relationship state, recall signals, and approved tags, while Reference lore and long scenes require direct relevance or host request. Foundation is an atomic budget requirement and cannot be silently omitted.
