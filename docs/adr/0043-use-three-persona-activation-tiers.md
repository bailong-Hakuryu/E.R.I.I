# Use three persona activation tiers

Approved Persona Interpretation assigns each item a Foundation, Situational, or Reference activation tier. Planned recall always carries Foundation identity, inner dynamics, tensions, hard boundaries, baseline voice rules, relationship premise, and their minimum formative dependency closure; Situational material, including source-backed Contextual Voice Patterns, is selected deterministically from the query, relationship state, recall signals, and source-typed Interaction Context Signals matched against approved conditions or tags, while Reference lore and long scenes require direct relevance or host request. Host-observed environment, core-derived relationship context, and evaluator-inferred emotional context remain distinct inputs, and the reply model cannot activate a pattern by self-declaring an emotion. A contextual expression example establishes that a register is available under supported conditions, not that its exact wording is a global catchphrase or habitual frequency. Foundation is an atomic budget requirement and cannot be silently omitted.
