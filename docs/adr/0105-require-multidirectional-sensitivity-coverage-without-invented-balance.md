# Require multidirectional sensitivity coverage without invented balance

A sensitivity compiler that searches mainly for fear, abandonment, violation
and pain can reduce a character to a trauma-response machine. Character
continuity also develops through fulfilled values, ordinary joys, first acts of
agency, being understood, successful trust and conflicts between values that
are all genuinely important. Positive wording alone is not sufficient, but
positive and agentic experiences must not be systematically invisible.

Reflection Sensitivity Profile compilation therefore checks five
non-exclusive source directions:

- `threat_or_violation`: a value, boundary, attachment or identity is threatened
  or violated;
- `fulfillment`: a grounded value, desire or ordinary-life aspiration is
  materially fulfilled;
- `agency_milestone`: the character makes a first or materially significant
  self-directed choice, refusal or commitment;
- `relationship_meaning`: trust, companionship, recognition, separation,
  reunion or another relationship meaning becomes significant;
- `value_tension`: two grounded character values pull toward incompatible
  choices.

Each proposed sensitivity preserves the exact Blueprint, Formative Experience,
Manifest claim or approved Growth references and the bounded causal link from
those sources to the trigger condition. Long-form experience may support a
proposal through an explicit evidence chain; the compiler cannot replace that
chain with a generic personality label.

Compilation also produces a non-authoritative Sensitivity Coverage Report. It
shows which directions have resolvable support, which are ambiguous and which
are absent. Missing coverage is not a validation error and does not authorize
the compiler to create a balancing trait. If a character's supplied source
material genuinely supports only some directions, the remaining directions
stay unknown until the owner supplies new Blueprint material or a later
relationship forms approved Growth.

The report is review assistance, not persona authority, a completeness score or
a psychological diagnosis. Approval applies to the exact profile proposal and
its source links, never to auto-generated filler.

## Consequences

Characters can be shaped by joy, agency and meaningful fulfillment as well as
pain, while the original source remains authoritative. Tests must include both
supported positive milestones and unsupported attempts to manufacture balance,
and import/export must preserve profile evidence without treating the coverage
report as a trigger input.
