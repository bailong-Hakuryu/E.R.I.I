# Interpret persona reflections only after event acceptance

E.R.I.I. will separate neutral relationship-event extraction from character-specific interpretation into two ordered contracts even when one model provider implements both. RelationshipEventExtractorV1 sees the Source Transcript and only the minimal factual or historical references needed to propose evidence-backed neutral events and qualitative signals; it cannot emit Persona Reflection text or use the Character Blueprint to make unsupported facts fit the character. Only after the deterministic adjudicator accepts a Relationship Event and applies its bounded rule-based state effects may PersonaReflectionInterpreterV1 receive that accepted event, its evidence, and an approved persona-and-relationship context. The interpreter returns a strict Persona Reflection Decision of either `kind=reflection` or `kind=no_reflection`; an accepted reflection becomes an independent Persona Reflection Record with minimal Reflection Context Provenance rather than event metadata. The interpreter cannot rewrite evidence, event facts, state, or Blueprint and cannot authorize Persona Growth. A missing, invalid, or failed reflection remains retryable or terminally partial without revoking the accepted event, while ordinary events may complete with an explicit no-reflection result instead of manufactured inner monologue.
