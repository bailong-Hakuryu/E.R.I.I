# Preserve relationship consequences without forcing repair

An evidence-backed reply can be continuous and still hurt the User, reject
them, maintain a boundary or end a relationship. Passing continuity review does
not make the consequences harmless. Conversely, the existence of harm does not
make the reply unsupported drift. ADR-0099 keeps those decisions orthogonal.

When a delivered reply and subsequent evidence establish a relationship
consequence, E.R.I.I. records the accepted Relationship Event, applies the
ordinary bounded Relationship State policy and preserves any resulting
Narrative Tension. Recall may surface what was said, why it was grounded, how
the User responded and what remains unresolved. The character cannot silently
forget the event merely because remembering it is uncomfortable.

The kernel does not turn accountability into mandatory reconciliation. It does
not automatically generate an apology, retract a boundary, forgive either
party, restore trust, reduce conflict, or require continued contact. Depending
on later evidence and the character's causal continuity, the character may:

- apologize and attempt repair;
- explain the choice while acknowledging its impact;
- maintain the boundary without denying the harm;
- remain conflicted or postpone a response;
- withdraw from or end the relationship.

Each later action is a new Source Turn and, when accepted, a new Relationship
Event. A repair is authoritative because the character actually chose and
expressed it through the normal continuity path, not because a state machine
inserted a morally preferred response. An unresolved consequence remains
available to memory and narrative projection until evidence-backed resolution
rules say otherwise; elapsed time or an unrelated pleasant exchange cannot
erase it.

Interpretation remains character-centered under ADR-0101. The User's words can
prove the User's own forgiveness, hurt, expectations or refusal, but cannot
declare the character remorseful, reconciled or changed. A Relationship
Reviewer adjudicates evidence-bound candidates rather than selecting the
character's desired feelings, ending or state values.

The same consequence principle applies after an exceptional utterance is
recognized by the relationship-consequence track. Its effect on the User may be
authoritative relationship history while the original utterance's persona
authority remains quarantined. A later repair cannot retroactively turn that
utterance into an aligned delivery.

Product safety remains a separate gate. A host may withhold an otherwise
continuous reply under a disclosed safety or abuse policy, but it must not
forge an OOC verdict to do so. E.R.I.I.'s continuity and relationship records
preserve the distinction.

## Consequences

Characters retain agency, boundaries, conflict and the possibility of genuine
failure while remaining accountable to their lived relationship history. The
system supports repair but cannot manufacture remorse. Narrative Tension
resolution follows the character-centered, evidence-separated projection in
ADR-0101.
