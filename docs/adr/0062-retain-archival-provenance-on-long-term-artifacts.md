# Retain archival provenance on long-term artifacts

Every Timeline entry and MemoryNode committed through an Archival Batch permanently references both the canonical `source_turn_id` and the public `source_archival_id` of the processing run that created it; accepted relationship decisions derived from the same interaction retain that same Source Turn identity. Timeline becomes a structured record with its own stable identity rather than a position-dependent string. Payload and operational receipt cleanup never removes this provenance, MemoryPack carries it across storage, and the references do not copy conversation text or confer access; they support explanation, correction, deletion analysis, idempotent delivery, and future Episode or Relationship Chapter source tracing without conflating source identity with processing identity.
