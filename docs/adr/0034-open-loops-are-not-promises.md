# Open loops are not promises

E.R.I.I. models an unfinished relationship matter that expects continuation as an Open Loop rather than a Promise when no party has accepted a concrete obligation. Open Loops close through append-only resolution events and only derive side-effect-free recall signals; legacy `MemoryNode.is_unresolved` records remain lower-authority compatibility sources with explicit provenance, are not promoted into relationship history, and no longer gain permanent salience merely because they are unresolved.
