# Activate contextual voice from sourced interaction signals

Runtime Contextual Voice Pattern selection will consume source-typed Interaction Context Signals rather than a reply model's self-declared state. `host_observed` signals describe only externally observable facts such as activity, environment, and communication modality; `core_derived` signals come from the current Persona Instance's accepted relationship baseline, state, events, and recall signals; `evaluator_inferred` signals may describe emotion or psychological context only when an independent evaluator cites the current User message or accepted history that supports the inference. Engine applies a deterministic, versioned matcher to approved pattern conditions and records a temporary Voice Pattern Activation with the pattern and supporting-signal references. The activation can inform reply generation and Continuity Evaluation but does not persist as personality, relationship history, or long-term memory, and the model cannot unlock a register by asserting an emotion after choosing the wording it wants to justify.
