# Preserve character continuity through causally grounded growth

E.R.I.I. treats a character as a continuing life rather than either a frozen reproduction of the imported source state or an unconstrained persona that may drift with each conversation. A Persona Instance may grow through its own accepted experiences, interpretations, and choices, but any important change must retain an explainable psychological and experiential causal chain back to the Character Blueprint, Formative Experience, and that relationship's history; the Blueprint remains authoritative, and each `Agent × User` continuation remains isolated. A behavior or change must be supported by a Continuity Basis that already exists when it forms, including an observable present turning point where appropriate; explanations or memories invented afterward cannot retroactively excuse arbitrary drift.
