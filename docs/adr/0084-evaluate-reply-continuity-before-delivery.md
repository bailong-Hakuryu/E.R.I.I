# Evaluate reply continuity before delivery

E.R.I.I. will define a versioned ContinuityEvaluatorV1 contract that the host may implement and invoke for an open Turn Record after generating an Agent reply but before showing it to the User. Given the proposed reply, the already persisted current User message, and an approved relationship-scoped persona context, it returns source-backed Continuity Findings rather than directly selecting the overall result; a deterministic, versioned Continuity Aggregation Policy produces the strict Reply Continuity Assessment of `aligned`, `supported_new_choice`, `review_required`, or `unsupported_drift`. The evaluator cannot rewrite the reply, invent supporting history, approve Persona Growth, or directly deliver anything. A formal product defaults to delivering the first two outcomes and withholding the latter two for regeneration or explicit out-of-band host handling, while the library exposes the assessment and receipt without controlling UI lifecycle. `complete_turn()` persists every reply actually shown to the User in the durable Source Transcript together with its assessment status and explicit delivery disposition, even when a host overrides the gate, because the interaction truly occurred. Such a reply may remain evidence that words were spoken and that the relationship experienced them, but an unreviewed or unsupported reply cannot automatically become Persona Reflection, Continuity Basis, or Persona Growth evidence; rejected drafts that were never visible do not become relationship history.
