# Persist reflection records with minimal context provenance

ADR-0013 already establishes that historical Persona Reflections cannot be regenerated or overwritten, so E.R.I.I. will complete that design without introducing a second full persona or relationship snapshot. Each accepted reflection becomes an immutable, relationship-scoped Persona Reflection Record with a stable `reflection_id`, a required reference to one accepted Relationship Event, its interpreter descriptor, recorded time, and minimal Reflection Context Provenance. That provenance retains the exact Source Turn and Evidence references, event identity, Blueprint ID and hash, Manifest revision, Relationship Baseline identity, approved growth revisions, and relevant prior event or reflection identities used at interpretation time; it freezes the causal basis through IDs, versions, and hashes rather than copying the complete Blueprint, transcript, prompt, or relationship history. Reflection Correction and Reinterpretation are typed append-only records that target an existing `reflection_id`, retain their own source and context provenance, and never mutate the original. Existing reflection strings stored in Relationship Event metadata migrate into deterministic legacy records linked to their event, while any unavailable historical context remains explicitly `legacy_unavailable` rather than being reconstructed from the character's current state.
